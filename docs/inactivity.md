# Inactivity

- A board is inactive when nobody has touched anything on it. After the **reset-available time** (default 2 minutes) the reset button becomes available to everyone; after the **auto-reset time** (default 10 minutes) the board resets itself.
- Both times are settings on `ChessArbiter`, configurable in the in-world settings UI, with per-board overrides like the other settings. 
- **Either time can be "never"** (stored as `-1`; `0` would mean immediately), e.g. for tournaments where a crashed player may need longer to get back.
  - Reset-available "never": nobody else can reset the board, however long it's idle.
  - Auto-reset "never": the board never resets itself.
  - When both are set to times, the reset-available time must not exceed the auto-reset time.
- With both set to "never", a board whose player has left for good stays as it is until an organizer resets it. So organizers can always reset a board regardless of the timers. They take ownership of the board and reset it directly (see [Ownership](ownership.md)).
- **Shared clock:** the last activity time is a synced field holding VRChat server time (`Networking.GetServerTimeInSeconds()`), so every client agrees on how long the board has been idle without syncing a timer. The `double` seconds version avoids the wraparound of the `int` milliseconds one. Every change the board sends stamps it, and so does a touch that changes nothing (below).
- **Touching anything on the board counts as activity**: picking up a piece (even dropping it back where it came from, or an illegal drop), stepping through a replay, pressing a board button. Each touch updates the synced timestamp: the player takes ownership and serializes, at most once every few seconds (throttled), since players fidget with pieces. During a game only the side to move's touches count, so the waiting player's can't overwrite the move being made (see [Implementation](#implementation)).
- **Exception to the ownership rule:** pressing reset means a non-player takes ownership while both players may still be active. `OnOwnershipRequest` allows the transfer once the board has been idle for at least the reset-available time, measured against the synced timestamp.
- **The automatic reset is done by the current owner only**, so it happens once. If the owner has left, VRChat has already passed ownership to someone else, who then does it.
- Applies whenever the board isn't in its reset state: a game in progress, an ended game, or a replay. A reset board has nothing to reset.
- A game ended by reset is logged as an unfinished game (PGN result `*`); see [Game endings](game-endings.md).

## Implementation

`ChessBoard` (8.5).

- `IsIdleForResetAt(now)` and `IsIdleForAutoResetAt(now)`: the board isn't reset, and the snapshot's reset-available or auto-reset time (not `Never`) has passed since `lastActivity`. The times come from the settings snapshot (`GameNumber`), so changing them on the arbiter applies to a board in use only after a re-snapshot or on its next game.
- **Reset button:** `CanResetAs(name, organizer)` also allows anyone once `IsIdleForResetAt(ServerTime())`, matching the ownership exception (`MayOwnAs`), so a bystander's reset is both allowed and able to take ownership.
- **Automatic reset:** the owner's `Update` calls `CheckAutoResetAt(now)` on every frame while the board isn't reset (after its flag-fall check). Once idle for the auto-reset time it resets as the reset button does (`ClearGameAt`: a game in progress is logged, on time if its flag has fallen, otherwise as unfinished), logs `[ChessBoard] <board>: reset after N s idle`, and sends `ChangeReset`. The reset board is then never idle, so it happens once.
- **Touches:** every piece input (`PiecePickedUp`, `PieceDropped`, `PromotionChosen`, `KingDroppedOnCastleSpace`, and the replay pickups and drops) first calls `TouchAsAt(name, organizer, now)`. A touch counts from the player who'd act next: in a game in progress outside replay, the side to move's player; in replay, whoever may work the replay controls. Elsewhere (the lobby, an ended game outside replay) pieces can't be moved, and touches don't count. A touch that counts stamps `lastActivity` and sends it as `ChangeActivity` (taking ownership), unless the last activity was less than `ActivityThrottle` (5 s) ago, in which case nothing is sent. A change that's sent anyway (a move) stamps the time too, which restarts the throttle.
  - **Why the other player's touches don't count during a game:** the player waiting for a move would fidget exactly while the opponent moves. Their touch would take ownership and send the position from before that move, and if it arrived after the move, the move would be lost. The side to move's own touches can't race their move, which comes from the same client.
  - `ChangeActivity` isn't told to the toucher's own listeners (nothing they show has changed); receivers are told it like any change, with `lastChangeReceived` set, and a view does nothing for it.
- **Debug view:** the text shows how long the board has been idle and when reset becomes available and the board resets itself, and how many times this client has sent the board (`ChessBoard.serializations`, counted in `OnPreSerialization`). **Fidget** picks up a piece every half second for 30 s and logs the pickups, the activity stamps and the sends; **Default auto-reset time** cycles the instance default through 60 s, 600 s and never. See [Testing](testing.md#debug-view).
- `BoardTest` gets `TestInactivity` (8.5): a bystander's reset before and after the reset-available time and with "never", the automatic reset (at its time and not before, once, logged, sent; "never", 0, an ended game, a fallen flag, the lobby), the throttle, who counts (side to move, the other player, bystanders, organizers, replay, lobby, ended), every piece input touching, and `ChangeActivity` sent and received. The owner-only call in `Update` needs real frames and clients.
